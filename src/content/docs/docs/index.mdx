OpenMoat checks each tool call your coding agent's hook reports before it runs, with one policy on your machine. Normal work goes through, what the policy has no rule for goes to your agent's own prompt, what outlives the session waits for you, and reading your keys or switching OpenMoat off never runs.

## Eight parts, one for each way an agent can do harm

<PartGrid />

One Rust binary for macOS, Linux and Windows; no AI model decides. Nothing leaves your machine unless you set up [phone approvals](/docs/remote-approvals/) or [audit delivery](/docs/see-what-happened/siem-delivery/). What no part stops: [Limits](/docs/limits/).
