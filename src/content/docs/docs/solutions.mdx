<Aside type="note" title="Two facts that apply on several pages">

`openmoat run` needs macOS 13 or later, or Linux. It refuses to start on macOS 12 and earlier and on Windows.

Antigravity CLI support is written from its documentation. It has not been checked against a running `agy`.

</Aside>
